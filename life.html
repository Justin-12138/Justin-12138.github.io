Skateboarding

The part of the week with no benchmark. I'm working on the Ollie and the Kickflip. I like the culture, and the fact that progress is impossible to fake. You either land it or you don't.

English, out loud

English is the long game. I can read documentation all day, but saying the same thought out loud to another person is harder. I'm working on speaking and listening for real conversations, not just exams. IELTS is somewhere ahead, not a deadline yet.

Working on — speaking & listening
